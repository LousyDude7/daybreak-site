How a morning runs

One morning, one stage at a time.

A morning in Daybreak, starting at 6:45. Each stage fills your screen with its own color, so you always know where you are.

6:45AM

  1. water_dropcheck

    Fuel

    A drink or a bite to start the day. There's no timer on fuel, so tap Done when you've finished.

    • Glass of water
    • Black coffee
    • Smoothie
  2. directions_runcheck

    Exercise

    A few minutes of movement to wake the body up. A dot paces every rep, and the stage ticks itself off at your goal.

    • Pushups
    • Squats
    • Jumping jacks
  3. aircheck

    Mindfulness

    A calm minute or two before the day gets busy. Breathe with the dot, or sit with a timer that keeps count for you.

    • Box breathing
    • Meditation
    • Body scan
  4. bedcheck

    Extra

    Any other habit you want in your morning, each with a countdown you can read from across the room. When the time is up, an alarm rings.

    • Make the bed
    • Cold shower
    • Sunlight
  5. checkcheck

    Done

    Every stage is done. Your streak goes up by one, and tomorrow's reminder is already set.

    • Streak +1

Set up in a minute

Build it
once.

Choose your stages, then set a start time and the days. Every morning after that, Daybreak has it ready.

  1. 1
    Choose your stages

    Start with fuel, exercise and mindfulness, then add up to two extras, like making the bed.

  2. 2
    Set the time and days

    A reminder arrives at your start time, and a nudge follows if you haven't started.

  3. 3
    Press start and follow

    Reps, breaths and timers are guided on screen. Your streak keeps count.
