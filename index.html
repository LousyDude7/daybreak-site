How a morning runs

Every stage gets the whole screen.

Each stage fills your phone with its own color, so you always know where you are. Reps follow a pacing dot, breathing follows a box, and timers count down in numbers you can read across the room.

  1. water_dropFuel · Glass of water

  2. directions_runExercise · Pushups, paced

  3. airMindfulness · Box breathing

  4. bedExtra · Make the bed

Set up in a minute

Build it
once.

Choose your stages, then set a start time and the days. Every morning after that, Daybreak has it ready.

  1. 1
    Choose your stages

    Start with fuel, exercise and mindfulness, then add up to two extras, like making the bed.

  2. 2
    Set the time and days

    A reminder arrives at your start time, and a nudge follows if you haven't started.

  3. 3
    Press start and follow

    Reps, breaths and timers are guided on screen. Your streak keeps count.
